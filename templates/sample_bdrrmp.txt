IV. Legal Basis of the BDRRM Plan
International: SENDAI Framework, Paragraph 33 — "National and local government shall prepare or review and
periodically update disaster preparedness and contingency policies, plans and programs."

National: RA 10121 (Philippine Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Act); NDRRMC-NSC JMC No. 1, 2016; RA
10821 (Children's Emergency Relief and Protection Act); RA 9729 (Climate Change Act); RA 10174 (People's Survival
Fund); DILG Memorandum Circulars related to DRRM; NEDA's "We Recover as One" Policy.

Local: Executive Order organizing the BDRRMC; Barangay Resolution adopting the BDRRM Plan; Barangay Ordinance
on BDRRM Fund utilization.
